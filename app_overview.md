# Pocket Bloom - App Overview

Live URL: https://bloom-save-sprout.lovable.app/

---

## What is Pocket Bloom?

A personal expense tracker that helps you:
- Track daily income and expenses
- See net cash flow at a glance
- Calculate guilt-free spending money
- Visualise spending patterns over time

---

## Smart Insights Formula

Guilt-Free Spending = Total Income - Fixed Commitments

Fixed commitments include:
- Rent or Housing
- EMI payments
- Family support
- Any recurring must-pay items
